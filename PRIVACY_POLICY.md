# Privacy Policy — Calculator (Calculator Vault)

_Last updated: 2 August 2026_

Calculator ("the app") is a calculator with an optional, PIN-protected
private vault for photos, videos and documents.

## Data collection

The app collects **no data whatsoever**. It contains no analytics, no
advertising SDKs, no crash reporters, and no network access — the app does
not request the Internet permission and cannot transmit anything.

## Where your content lives

Everything you place in the vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored
**only on your device**, inside the app's private storage. Encryption keys
are held in your device's hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore /
iOS Keychain) and never leave the device. Your PIN and recovery key are never
stored — only salted cryptographic hashes.

## Backups

Device backups are intentionally disabled for this app. Because encryption
keys cannot leave your device's secure hardware, a backup copy of the vault
would be unreadable; disabling backups also means your encrypted content is
never uploaded anywhere. **If you uninstall the app or lose the device, vault
contents cannot be recovered.**

## Biometrics

If you enable fingerprint/face unlock, authentication is performed entirely
by the operating system. The app never sees or stores biometric data.

## Permissions

- **Biometric permission** — used only for optional vault unlock.
- Photo/video import uses the system photo picker, which does not require
  media permissions.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.
